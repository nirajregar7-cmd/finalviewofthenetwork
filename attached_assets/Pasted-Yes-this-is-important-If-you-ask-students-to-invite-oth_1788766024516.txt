Yes — this is important. If you ask students to invite others, they need a clear reason to do it. For The Network, I’d make the reward community/status + useful platform benefits, rather than immediately giving money.

🎁 What students get by inviting others

When someone taps Invite, show:

Invite students. Grow your network. Unlock benefits.

For example:

Invite 3 students
→ 🏅 Early Connector badge

Invite 10 students
→ ⭐ Founding Network Member recognition

Invite 25 students
→ 🚀 Community Builder badge

Invite 50 students
→ 👑 Top Connector recognition

You can also add:

🏆 Leaderboard recognition — Top inviters of the week/month
🎖️ Special profile badge — visible on their profile/posts
🌐 Community Builder status — especially for people helping their college community grow
📜 Certificate/LOR eligibility — if they genuinely contribute to building a community, not simply spam invites
🔓 Early access to new features
🎯 Points/reputation that can later unlock platform benefits
But there's an even better reward for The Network

Don't make the message:

“Invite 10 friends and get a badge.”

Make the core motivation:

“Your network becomes more useful when the people you know are here.”

So after inviting:

“You invited 8 students 🎉”

You’re helping build the LPU community.

8 students joined through you.
Keep growing your network!

Then:

[ Invite more students ]

For Campus Ambassadors

This should be a separate system.

If an ambassador brings students and actually helps build an active LPU community, they can get:

🏅 LPU Community Builder / Founding Ambassador recognition
📜 Certificate + LOR
💼 Community-building experience
🚀 Opportunity to work more closely with The Network
⭐ Recognition on the platform

And importantly, don't reward ambassadors purely for raw signups. Otherwise people will spam links and you'll get fake/low-quality users.

Reward things like:

Students joined → Students activated → Posts/connections → Community activity

That aligns the ambassador's incentives with your actual goal: building a useful LPU community, not just collecting downloads.
