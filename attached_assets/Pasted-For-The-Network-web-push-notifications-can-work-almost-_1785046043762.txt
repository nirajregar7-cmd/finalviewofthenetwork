How it works
User opens The Network.

The app asks:

Allow notifications?

User taps Allow.
The browser gives your app a unique notification token.
You save that token in your database.
Whenever something happens, your server sends a notification through Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
Google delivers it instantly to the user's device.

Even if Chrome is closed, the notification can still appear on Android as long as the browser's background services are available and the user hasn't disabled notifications.

Best practices

Don't send notifications for every small event. Prioritize notifications that bring users back because they involve people or deadlines.

For example:

Someone messaged you.
Someone accepted your request.
A student from another college wants to connect.
Your hackathon team is waiting for you.
A campus event starts in 30 minutes.

Avoid sending notifications like:

"Welcome back!"
"Open the app today!"
Random marketing messages every day.
