THE CORE IDEA:

The Network is for ALL students, not only engineering students.

A student should NOT be forced into one "stream".

We need to separate:

1. Degree / Program
2. Field / Specialization
3. Interests
4. Goals
5. Communities

These should be stored separately and used together to personalize the student's experience.

Psychology + Writing + Startups

Finance + Entrepreneurship + Product

5. PROFILE SETUP EXPERIENCE
--------------------------------------------------

Do not make onboarding feel like a long form.

Use a simple progressive 4-step setup:

STEP 1 — EDUCATION

"What are you studying?"

College
Degree / Program
Field / Specialization
Year / Semester

STEP 2 — INTERESTS

"What are you interested in?"

Select up to 8 interests.

STEP 3 — GOALS

"What are you looking to do?"

Select multiple goals.

STEP 4 — COMMUNITIES

"Find your communities"

Recommend communities based on:

- College
- Degree
- Field
- Interests
- Goals

Examples:

/nit-trichy
/ai-ml
/startups
/dsa
/hackathons
/research

Allow users to join communities immediately.

Also provide:

"Skip for now"

so users can complete their profile later.

FINAL EXPERIENCE
--------------------------------------------------

The student joins The Network.

They tell us:

"Who are you?"
→ Education

"What are you interested in?"
→ Interests

"What do you want to achieve?"
→ Goals

"Where do you want to belong?"
→ Communities

Then The Network responds:

"Here are the people, communities, resources and opportunities relevant to you."

The goal is:

DISCOVER → LEARN → CONNECT → BUILD → GROW
