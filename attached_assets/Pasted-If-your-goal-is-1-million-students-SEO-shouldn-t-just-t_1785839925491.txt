15. My Biggest SEO Suggestion

Don't think of yourself as one website.

Think of yourself as building India's student directory.

Imagine having:

1,000 college pages
10,000 community pages
100,000 student profiles
5,000 opportunity pages
2,000 event pages
10,000 blog posts

That's over 128,000 indexable pages. Each page can target a different search intent, creating a compounding traffic engine over time.

One thing I'd change

Right now you're describing The Network as a social platform.

For SEO, position it as a discovery platform.

People search for:

"AI community"
"Hackathons"
"Internships"
"Placement preparation"
"Coding clubs"
"College events"

They don't search for "student social network."

So your SEO should revolve around helping students discover communities, opportunities, people, and events. The social networking becomes what they experience after they arrive. That's how you build sustainable organic traffic.
