---
name: Reshared post engagement
description: Product rule for votes, reactions, and comments across reshares.
---

Reshares share one engagement thread rooted at the original post. Preserve its poll votes and comments instead of starting an empty thread; include historical engagement already attached to reshare records.

**Why:** The user expects reshares to keep previous poll votes and all comments visible, like other social apps.

**How to apply:** When changing feeds, reshare creation, poll voting, reactions, or comments, read and write shared engagement through the original post and avoid clearing engagement in reshare responses.
